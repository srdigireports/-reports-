01 · The Month in Focus
Executive Summary

AI visibility surging — the groundwork is paying off

May 2026 was a month of strong directional signals. All-channel revenue grew across virtually every property — Hotel June Malibu posted a standout +83.25% all-channel MoM revenue surge, Hotel June (portfolio) climbed +43.4% MoM, Santa Monica Proper delivered +16.41% MoM, and DTLA Proper's all-channel revenue nearly doubled YoY (+92.4%). The Proper Hotels portfolio all-users revenue crossed $2.38M in May, up +11.91% from April and +39.87% vs May 2025.

On the organic layer, six of nine tracked properties grew organic sessions MoM — led by SMP (+21.14%), HJM (+30.26%), APS (+9.29%), HJWL (+13.01%), DTLP (+9.64%), and AUP (+4.41%). DTLA Proper and Avalon Palm Springs both turned in meaningful organic revenue gains MoM (+13.18% and +39.94% respectively). Where organic revenue softened, it did so against strong April baselines and a category-wide AI search transition that is structurally redistributing first-click intent — the leading indicators tell a more optimistic story.

The biggest May story is AI visibility. Gains this month were portfolio-wide and substantial: Austin Proper surged +11.5pp to 52% visibility, SF Proper climbed +9.5pp to 45%, Santa Monica Proper hit +7.2pp to 64%, and DTLA Proper gained +6.3pp to 30%. SOV gains are tracking even faster, with AUP at 53% (+13.1pp). These are leading indicators — AI visibility gains at this pace typically translate to organic session recovery within 60–90 days as generative citations compound into direct navigational and branded search lifts.
